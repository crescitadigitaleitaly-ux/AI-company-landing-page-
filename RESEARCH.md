# Research and creative decisions

Research performed 30 October 2026. Firecrawl was used for the publicly accessible job and five supplied references. No client was contacted, no proposal was submitted and no Upwork Connects were spent.

## Job evidence

https://www.upwork.com/jobs/~022108543278338218261

The public job requests a professional landing page for a venture-backed industrial software / physical AI startup. The accessible listing does not identify a company or provide verified product details. Consequently, all company-specific narrative here is original fictional concept content. Reference-site claims and customer logos were not copied onto this site.

## Brand checks

A search for “NEXORA AI” returned real technology-company listings, including https://uk.linkedin.com/company/nexora-ai and https://www.nexoraaitech.com/. VELTRIQ and Orynth also showed existing AI businesses, so they were rejected. The final “AERVEK technology” search returned no obvious technology-company match. This is a basic conflict check, not trademark clearance. AERVEK remains explicitly fictional.

## Privacy and publication

The browser-only form validates sample input and simulates success. There is no backend, external request, persistence or delivery. No cookies or analytics are added. A privacy dialog accurately explains the demo behavior and potential host logging. This is not a legal-compliance certification.

The owner confirmed https://mouzzambuild.com/ during review; the footer credit links there. No canonical URL, sitemap URL, business email or social account has been invented. The demo blocks indexing. Publishing is intentionally paused until explicit owner approval, as requested by the brief.
